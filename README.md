# Early-Career Developer Salary Prediction

**Mission:** To empower young people with the skills, guidance and opportunities to build successful careers.
**Problem:** Early-career developers often can't tell what their skills and experience are worth, so they struggle to negotiate fairly and choose what to learn next.
**Solution:** A model trained on the Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2025 that estimates annual developer salary from country, experience, education, company size and work arrangement, for young developers and their mentors.
